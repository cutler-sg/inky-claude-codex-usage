## Generated illustrations

`designs/artwork/`, `designs/integrated-v2/`, and `assets/live/` preserve the generated
illustrations and clean plates created for this project with built-in ImageGen.
Exact prompts and provenance are retained in their JSON manifests and PNG metadata.
The generated-image filenames are provenance identifiers, not credentials.
The numbered study snapshots are frozen illustrative usage data; live usage stays
under ignored `state/` and is never included in the release.
